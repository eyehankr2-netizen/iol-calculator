Formulas

SRK/T
Retzlaff JA, Sanders DR, Kraff MC. J Cataract Refract Surg 1990;16:333–340; erratum 1990;16:528. Corrected axial length threshold 24.2 mm. When the corneal-height square-root term is negative it is set to zero (Haigis W. J Cataract Refract Surg 1993;19:442–446, with the authors' reply).
Hoffer Q
Hoffer KJ. J Cataract Refract Surg 1993;19:700–712; errata 1994;20:677 and 2007;33:2–3. Axial length is limited to 18.5–31.0 mm for the ACD prediction.
Holladay 1
Holladay JT, Prager TC, Chandler TY, et al. J Cataract Refract Surg 1988;14:17–24.
SRK II
Sanders DR, Retzlaff J, Kraff MC. J Cataract Refract Surg 1988;14:136–141. Refraction factor 1.25 when the emmetropia power is above 14 D, otherwise 1.0.

Lens constants

When only an A-constant is entered, the other constants are derived from it: SRK/T ACD constant = 0.62467×A − 68.747; Hoffer Q pACD = 0.58357×A − 63.896; Holladay 1 SF = 0.5663×A − 65.60. Lens-specific optimized pACD and SF values are preferred when available.

Privacy and safety

No patient data is stored. K, axial length and results are cleared by New patient or when the app is closed. Only the IOL model list and preferences are saved on this device.

This tool supports, but does not replace, clinical judgment. Verify biometry and results. The final IOL choice is the surgeon's responsibility.
